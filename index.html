      About

      I am an AI & Data Science undergraduate at IIITDM Kurnool, currently in my third year, with a strong focus on deep learning, machine learning, and Python-based model development.

      I have hands-on experience designing, training, and evaluating neural networks — from classical frameworks like Scikit-learn, XGBoost, and LightGBM to building and training generative architectures such as diffusion models, flow-matching transformers, and GPT-style LLMs from scratch in PyTorch.

      I am proficient in end-to-end ML/DL workflows — from data preprocessing and feature engineering to model experimentation, hyperparameter tuning, and deployment. Recently, I pretrained PM-MiniFinLLM, a 110M-parameter GPT-2-style finance LLM trained on SEC EDGAR data, followed by SFT and DPO alignment.

      I've also expanded deeply into the LLM application space — building intelligent agents and multi-step pipelines using LangChain and LangGraph, and designing Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems (including a legal RAG pipeline over the Indian Penal Code and Constitution, evaluated with Ragas) that ground language models in external knowledge sources.

      Most recently, I've been fine-tuning FLUX.2-Klein-9B using QLoRA on AWS SageMaker, working through the internals of Euler ODE solvers and patchify/unpatchify operations.

      Beyond core technical work, I have a strong interest in finance and psychology, which shapes how I think about modeling real-world systems, human behavior, and decision-making. I enjoy collaborating, learning new tools quickly, and contributing to projects — from deep learning pipelines to agentic AI systems — that aim to create measurable impact.
